Small stomachs

Why nutrient density matters

Small kids have small stomachs and big nutrient needs, so every bite has to work harder than an adult's does. A dense snack fills a real gap on the days the vegetables lose.

Parent questions

The bits parents ask first.

Is it safe for kids with nut allergies?+

Yes. NutriBrick is nut-free. The bind comes from sunflower seed butter, not peanut or tree nut butter.

How long do the bricks keep?+

Up to 5 days in the fridge, or 2 months in the freezer.

What flavours are there?+

Chocolate and cinnamon at launch. One flavour, done properly, before we add more.

Does it replace a meal?+

No. It is a nutrient-dense snack that sits between meals, not instead of them.

Why we made it

A Year 10 idea, built properly.
